🍫 Chocolate Cake

Prep Time 45 mins

Servings 8

Difficulty Medium

  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.
  3. Whisk wet ingredients separately.
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
  5. Pour batter into a greased pan.
  6. Bake for 30-35 minutes. Let cool and serve.

🍝 Pasta Alfredo

Prep Time 30 mins

Servings 4

Difficulty Easy

  • 200g fettuccine pasta
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp garlic (minced)
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  1. Cook pasta until al dente.
  2. Melt butter, add garlic and sauté.
  3. Add cream and simmer for 5 mins.
  4. Stir in Parmesan and cooked pasta.
  5. Season and serve hot.

🍗 Butter Chicken

Prep Time 40 mins

Servings 4

Difficulty Medium

  • 500g boneless chicken
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup tomato puree
  • ½ cup cream
  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, spices, and garlic paste for 1 hour.
  2. Cook chicken until golden.
  3. In a pan, melt butter and add tomato puree.
  4. Add cream and simmer.
  5. Mix in chicken and cook 10 more mins.
  6. Serve hot with naan or rice.

🍚 Vegetable Pulao

Prep Time 15 mins

Servings 4

Difficulty Easy

  • 1 cup Basmati rice (soaked)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup mixed veggies (peas, carrots)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 bay leaves & cloves
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  1. Heat ghee in a pot, add cumin and whole spices.
  2. Sauté onions until golden brown.
  3. Add mixed vegetables and cook for 2 mins.
  4. Add soaked rice and water. Season with salt.
  5. Cover and cook on low heat for 15 mins.
  6. Fluff rice with a fork and serve hot.

🧀 Palak Paneer

Prep Time 25 mins

Servings 3

Difficulty Medium

  • 200g Paneer (cubed)
  • 2 bunches Spinach (washed)
  • 1 onion & 1 tomato (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 2 tbsp fresh cream
  1. Blanch spinach in boiling water, then puree.
  2. Sauté onion, ginger-garlic paste, and tomato.
  3. Add spices (turmeric, chili, garam masala).
  4. Pour in spinach puree and simmer for 5 mins.
  5. Add paneer cubes and fresh cream. Mix gently.
  6. Serve hot with naan or roti.

Prep Time 10 mins

Servings 4

Difficulty Easy

  • 2 cups Chickpeas (boiled/canned)
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp Chana Masala powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • Coriander leaves for garnish
  1. Heat oil, add onions, and cook until brown.
  2. Add tomato puree and cook until oil separates.
  3. Stir in Chana Masala powder, cumin, and salt.

  5. Cover and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  6. Garnish with coriander and serve.

🥣 Gulab Jamun

Prep Time 40 mins

Servings 10

Difficulty Medium

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp ghee (clarified butter)
  • Milk (for kneading)
  • 1 cup sugar & 1 cup water (syrup)
  • Cardamom powder & Rose water
  1. Mix milk powder, flour, and ghee. Knead with milk.
  2. Form small, smooth balls from the dough.
  3. Deep fry balls on low heat until golden brown.
  4. Boil sugar, water, and cardamom to make syrup.
  5. Soak fried balls in hot syrup for 2 hours.
  6. Serve warm or cold.

🥕 Gajar Ka Halwa

Prep Time 45 mins

Servings 4

Difficulty Easy

  • 4 cups carrots (grated)
  • 2 cups full-fat milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1/4 cup nuts (cashews/almonds)
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  1. Sauté grated carrots in 1 tbsp ghee for 5 mins.
  2. Add milk and cook until milk evaporates.
  3. Add sugar and continue cooking until thickened.
  4. Add remaining ghee, cardamom, and nuts.
  5. Roast for 2-3 more minutes.
  6. Serve hot garnished with chopped nuts.

🍮 Rice Kheer

Prep Time 50 mins

Servings 5

Difficulty Easy

  • 1/4 cup Basmati rice (washed)
  • 1 liter full-fat milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tbsp chopped almonds/pistachios
  • Few strands of saffron
  1. Boil milk in a heavy-bottomed pan.
  2. Add washed rice to the boiling milk.
  3. Simmer on low heat, stirring often, for 30 mins.
  4. Once rice is soft and milk thickens, add sugar.
  5. Add cardamom, saffron, and nuts. Mix well.
  6. Cool in the fridge and serve chilled.
